# KeyCoin: Neon Metropolis 3150

## Setting
The year is 3150. Humanity has spread across floating megacities orbiting Earth and mined colonies on asteroids. Money is obsolete—KeyCoin, a quantum crypto-coin, governs access to everything: energy, AI services, housing, and even personal status. But KeyCoin is not just currency—it’s sentient and evolves with its holders, granting powers based on one’s role and expertise.

## Core Concept
Players compete and cooperate to dominate the neon cyber-metropolis using KeyCoin, which powers abilities, unlocks infrastructure, and affects the city’s AI-controlled environment. Your role defines your abilities, playstyle, and interactions with KeyCoin.

## Roles & Abilities

### Computing Architect
- **Quantum Hack:** Temporarily override city systems.
- **KeyCoin Sync:** Link KeyCoin to AI entities for passive resource gain.
- **Special:** Create “Data Constructs,” temporary AI minions.

### Nano-Engineer
- **Nano Swarm:** Deploy nanobots to repair or damage.
- **Key Infusion:** Embed KeyCoin into tech to boost efficiency.
- **Special:** Build portable mini-factories.

### Cybernetic Operative
- **Phase Cloak:** Temporarily invisible or intangible.
- **KeyCoin Disruptor:** Drain KeyCoin from enemy networks.
- **Special:** “Neural Jack” to steal abilities.

### Bio-Integrator
- **Symbiotic Merge:** Merge with drones or beasts for boosts.
- **KeyCoin Growth:** Evolve bio-constructs with enhanced traits.
- **Special:** Bio-terraforming abilities.

### Quantum Trader
- **Coin Flux:** Shift KeyCoin value in zones.
- **Smart Contract:** Force temporary agreements.
- **Special:** Invest KeyCoin to generate rare items or events.

## Unique Mechanics
- **KeyCoin Evolution:** KeyCoin “learns” and grants new abilities based on usage.
- **Role Synergy:** Abilities can combine between different roles.
- **Tech vs Biology:** Choice between synthetic tech and bio-based abilities affects city interactions.
